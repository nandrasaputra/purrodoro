# Play Store Feature Graphic Design

## Goal

Create one polished Google Play feature graphic that immediately communicates
Purrodoro's core promise: **Plan. Focus. Feel progress.**

## Deliverable

- Output: `assets/playstore/feature_graphic.png`
- Size: 1024 × 500 pixels
- Format: opaque RGB PNG
- Generator: `assets/playstore/generate_store_portraits_no_ui.py`

## Composition

Use a horizontal split-hero layout:

- Left side: Purrodoro brand mark, main headline, and compact feature line.
- Right side: one large official Purrodoro mascot pose with simple feature
  symbols.
- Keep all critical text and mascot details at least 64 pixels from each edge.

## Content

- Brand: `PURRODORO`
- Headline: `Plan. Focus. Feel progress.`
- Feature line: `TIMER · TASKS · HABITS · SOUNDS`

No app UI, screenshots, phone frames, gradients, or photographic scenery.

## Visual System

- Cream base background.
- Peach and lavender geometric background shapes.
- Coral focus ring and accents.
- Cocoa headline and supporting text.
- Nunito typography from the Android app resources.
- Existing official mascot artwork, preserving its original proportions and
  illustration style.

## Feature Symbols

Use small, flat symbols around the mascot:

- Timer/focus ring.
- Task checkmark.
- Habit streak marker.
- Sound waveform.

Symbols support the mascot and must not compete with the headline.

## Validation

- Exact 1024 × 500 dimensions.
- Opaque RGB output.
- Readable headline at reduced thumbnail size.
- No clipped text or mascot details.
- Strong contrast between copy and background.
- Visually consistent with the final portrait screenshot campaign.
