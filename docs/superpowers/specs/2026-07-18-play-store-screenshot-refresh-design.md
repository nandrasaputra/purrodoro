# Play Store Screenshot Refresh

## Goal

Replace AI-heavy watercolor marketing art with product-led compositions that
still preserve Purrodoro's cozy mascot identity.

## Direction

- Use real app UI as the largest visual element.
- Use one existing official mascot asset per image as a supporting accent.
- Use flat cream, coral, and lavender fields with minimal geometric decoration.
- Use Nunito typography to match the Android app.
- Keep each headline to five words or fewer.
- Use one repeated 16:9 grid across all three images.
- Avoid generated scenery, glowing particles, fake device perspective, and
  decorative objects unrelated to app behavior.

## Image Set

1. **Plan. Focus. Grow.** — product overview.
2. **Focus without pressure** — timer and cycle controls.
3. **Turn plans into progress** — tasks and categories.
4. **Build habits that stick** — habits and streaks.
5. **Find your perfect sound** — ambience and lo-fi.
6. **See every focused day** — Journey and history.

## Composition

- Output: 1080 × 1920 PNG in 9:16 portrait.
- Each image contains a finished, faithful static reconstruction of its Android
  feature using production copy, tokens, data shapes, and shipped artwork.
- Feature panels may show a full surface or a focused crop such as a timer,
  task card, habit streak, sound tile, or chart.
- Headline remains dominant at Play Store thumbnail size.
- New mascot poses may be generated from official character references, while
  preserving cream fur, three forehead stripes, mouthless face, and proportions.
- Mascot and highlighted UI share lower two-thirds of composition.
- Shared corner radius, border weight, shadow, and spacing across all images.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every image reads clearly at small Play Store thumbnail size.
- No manual screenshot replacement is required.
- UI labels, values, controls, and feature structure closely match Android code.
- Mascot appearance stays consistent with shipped Android artwork.
- No invented app feature or misleading UI.
- Set feels handcrafted and cohesive rather than generative.
